01 / A garden beyond its walls.

Project overview

Space / Memory / Interpretation

From classical garden
to digital landscape.

The project reinterprets the garden as a walkable environment. Courtyards give way to rockery, pavilions and cloud-filled views, turning spatial sequence into a form of storytelling.

My work connects visual research with scene and terrain design, interactive prototyping, and exhibition presentation.

02 / Establishing the space.

Blockouts explore massing, levels, water and circulation before detailed environment art.

03 / Shaping the atmosphere.

Rockery, architecture, vegetation and light build a sequence of intimate spaces and expansive views.

04 / Entering the realm.

In-game screenshots show the garden from the player’s perspective, with the interface preserved.

05 / Giving the world a character.

Concept exploration develops shape, expression and a visual identity for the project.
